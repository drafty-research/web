Drafty (updates incoming)

A Smarter Wiki For Data

Drafty strives to keep data up-to-date by recruiting people to review it from the crowd of people already using it. This continual review allows the maintenance of data to be self-sustaining over time. Potential reviewers can freely edit specific data, add new rows, delete rows, comment on rows, and see rankings and insights from our "open" rankings.

We are under construction with some new updates!

Datasets and Products

  • Computer Science Professors

    Used for an analysis of computer science hiring trends.

  • CS Open Rankings

    A new approach to computer science rankings. A meta ranker using real-time data from CS Professors as one of its sources.
